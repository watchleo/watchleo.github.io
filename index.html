Retro Music Player Importer

Add your own songs (any name, any format) and optional .lrc lyrics. Everything happens in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

Needs the Retro Music Player add-on. This tool only builds a songs pack — the music, titles and lyrics. Download the add-on from CurseForge and install it first.
Drop songs, .lrc files, a .zip or a whole folder here
or tap to choose files (mp3, wav, flac, m4a, ogg … + .lrc).
Internet is needed once to convert mp3/wav/etc. (Vorbis .ogg files skip that).
Tip: end names with a number to pick the disc — Song 5.mp3 + Song 5.lrc → disc 5.

How to install
  1. Install the Retro Music Player add-on from CurseForge (once).
  2. Click Build my songs pack and download the file.
  3. Open the downloaded .mcaddon with Minecraft.
  4. On your world, enable the Songs packs and keep the Retro Music Player packs on, then fully restart the world.

The last number in a file name picks the disc (My Song 7.mp3 → #7); files without one take the next free disc. You can change the disc number in the list. Max 24. A song you import replaces that disc's built-in song; discs you don't fill keep the add-on's own song, or stay silent if it has none. To change your songs later, build a new songs pack and install it over the old one. Only use music you own or have permission to use.
