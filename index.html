Retro Music Player Importer

Add your own songs (any name, any format) and optional .lrc lyrics. Everything happens in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

Drop songs, .lrc files, a .zip or a whole folder here
or tap to choose files (mp3, wav, flac, m4a, ogg … + .lrc).
Internet is needed once to convert mp3/wav/etc. (Vorbis .ogg files skip that).
Tip: end names with a number to pick the disc — Song 5.mp3 + Song 5.lrc → disc 5.

How to install
  1. Click Build my addon and download the file.
  2. Open the downloaded .mcaddon with Minecraft.
  3. Enable both packs on your world, then fully restart the world.

The last number in a file name picks the disc (My Song 7.mp3 → #7); files without one take the next free disc. You can change the disc number in the list. Max 24. Discs you don't fill stay empty (silent). Only use music you own or have permission to use.
